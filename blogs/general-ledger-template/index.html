General Ledger Template: Posting References and Reconciliation

A general ledger groups posted transactions by account. A useful template records the date, journal reference, explanation, debit, credit and running balance, then ties the closing account to independent evidence and the trial balance.

All amounts and operating results below are original fictional teaching examples in USD. They are not customer results, industry averages or forecasts. Use your own dated records and applicable accounting policies.

Use one account identity and one balance convention

The downloadable HTML template is editable and printable. It includes a completed Cash ledger and a blank working record with evidence and reviewer fields. It does not automatically calculate, post journals, create a trial balance or save edits. This limitation is deliberate: users must verify each result rather than assuming a blank manual template is an accounting application.

Set the account name/number, period, currency, opening balance and normal side. For a debit-balance account, running balance equals opening balance + debits − credits. A credit-balance account can instead use opening credit balance + credits − debits. Label the sign convention. Debit does not universally mean money received, and credit does not universally mean a cash payment.

A cash-only record cannot replace a complete general ledger. Keep document references separate from journal references when the two identify different evidence.

For a nonzero opening balance, retain evidence that it equals the prior period’s reviewed closing account. An unexplained opening balance can make every subsequent row appear orderly while the account remains wrong.

The receivable closes at $2,200 − $1,600 = $600. Revenue less the included expense is $1,950. Assets are $4,550 + $600 + $1,800 = $6,950; equity is $5,000 + $1,950 = $6,950, with no liabilities in this case. Equal trial-balance totals still require evidence and classification review.

Match Cash to a bank reconciliation, the receivable control to customer detail and Equipment to the asset register. A bank statement is not evidence for every GL account. Document and resolve differences rather than making unsupported entries to match a total.

Maintain references and corrections as the ledger grows

Use unique journal references, preserve the original entry and show the approved correcting or reversing entry. Keep dates and account names consistent. A sorted display is not an audit trail if someone can silently replace prior amounts. In a live system, access control, backups and period locks need a separate design.

Use the bookkeeping workflow for the monthly evidence package and the accounting cycle for adjusting and closing. Customer detail and supplier detail support their respective control accounts. This template does not choose the recognition policy for every transaction.

Frequently asked questions

Is the ledger the same as the journal?

No. The journal records entries in sequence; the ledger groups their postings by account.

Does this template calculate automatically?

No. It is an editable printable HTML record. Verify running balances and totals independently.

Can I reconcile every account to the bank?

No. Different accounts require different evidence, such as customer detail or an asset register.

Does a debit always mean a cash receipt?

No. Debit and credit effects depend on the account and transaction.
