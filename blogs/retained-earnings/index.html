Retained Earnings: Reconcile Profit, Dividends and Equity

Reconcile opening retained earnings, net income and declared dividends. Separate cash, contributed capital and any supported opening-balance adjustments.

Retained earnings is a cumulative equity balance built from earnings retained in the corporation, adjusted for distributions and any separately supported opening-balance items. It is not a bank account or a promise that the balance can be distributed.

All amounts and operating results below are original fictional teaching examples in USD. They are not customer results, industry averages or forecasts. Use your own dated records and applicable accounting policies.

Identify the corporation and the balance being reconciled

Retained earnings belongs within corporate shareholders’ equity. OpenStax’s equity comparison distinguishes the retained-earnings movement from the broader statement of changes in equity. A sole proprietor’s owner-capital record or drawings account should not be relabeled automatically as the corporate dividend example below.

Use the same entity, reporting period and currency for the opening balance, net income and distributions. The opening retained-earnings figure should agree to the previous closing ledger and financial statements, subject to any separately justified restatement. Net income is the period’s accounting result after its included income-tax expense; it is not revenue, EBITDA or customer collections. Verify the source statement rather than inserting a desired target profit.

Build one original retained-earnings rollforward

This original fictional USD case starts with $32,000 retained earnings. The corporation reports $12,000 net income for the period and validly declares $5,000 cash dividends. Assume no stock dividends, treasury-share transactions, opening corrections or other retained-earnings movements in the base case. The example assumes the declaration has been appropriately authorized; it does not assess legal distribution capacity.

Base corporate retained-earnings bridge; USD
MovementAmount
Opening retained earnings32,000
Current-period net income+12,000
Cash dividends declared in period−5,000
Closing retained earnings39,000

Closing retained earnings is $32,000 + $12,000 − $5,000 = $39,000. A positive $7,000 change in this account reflects these modeled income and distribution movements. It does not mean cash increased by $7,000. Keep each input traceable to its ledger, closing journal or board record, with sign conventions shown.

Use declaration and payment dates without counting twice

OpenStax’s cash-dividend entries distinguishes the declared obligation from later payment. In this case, declaration reduces retained earnings by $5,000 and creates Dividends Payable $5,000. If the system first uses a temporary Dividends account, its closing transfer must produce the same retained-earnings reduction, rather than a second $5,000 deduction.

Suppose $3,000 is paid during the period and $2,000 remains payable. The payment debits Dividends Payable $3,000 and credits Cash $3,000. It does not reduce retained earnings again. A retained-earnings schedule that subtracts $5,000 declared and another $3,000 paid would incorrectly show $36,000 rather than $39,000. Review both the authorization and settlement trail. Cash dividends are distributions, not operating expenses used to calculate the $12,000 net income.

Reconcile available cash through its own movements

Closing cash is $23,000 while retained earnings is $39,000. Operating cash differs from net income because receipts, payments and noncash or accrual items can occur on different dates. The equipment payment changes cash and the asset record, while share proceeds are an owner contribution. Neither becomes an extra retained-earnings credit merely because money entered or left the bank.

The unpaid $2,000 dividend remains a liability and belongs in future payment planning. This cash schedule is a limited reconciliation, not a complete statement of cash flows or proof that every balance-sheet account is correct. Verify restricted balances and commitments before describing any amount as usable.

Reconcile retained earnings inside total equity

Assume opening paid-in capital is $70,000 and opening accumulated other comprehensive income is $1,200. New shares add $10,000 paid-in capital, while a separately measured current-period OCI loss of $400 reduces that accumulated component to $800. The OCI amount is a supplied assumption for this bridge; this article does not decide its underlying classification.

The total bridge is $103,200 + $12,000 net income − $400 OCI + $10,000 share capital − $5,000 declared dividends = $119,800. Crediting share proceeds to retained earnings would misclassify equity even if total equity still agreed. A balanced accounting equation alone does not prove the component classification is right.

Keep any opening-balance correction separate and supported

An error affecting issued prior statements requires an applicable-framework and materiality assessment. It is not a general permission to post unexplained differences to retained earnings. SEC Staff Accounting Bulletin 108 discusses how prior-year errors can affect current financial statements for SEC registrants; that scope must not be treated as a universal bookkeeping shortcut.

In an independent alternative, assume a completed accounting assessment requires a $3,000 net-of-tax reduction to opening retained earnings. Show the previously reported $32,000, the separately labeled −$3,000 correction and adjusted opening $29,000. Adding $12,000 current net income and deducting $5,000 declared dividends then gives $36,000. Do not combine that alternative with the base $39,000 result or hide its effect inside this period’s expenses. Document the actual treatment and disclosures required.

Interpret a deficit without turning it into a cash measure

A debit retained-earnings balance is often described as an accumulated deficit. For a separate fictional case, opening retained earnings of $4,000 plus a $9,000 net loss and no distributions produces a $5,000 deficit. The arithmetic identifies the equity balance; it does not establish insolvency, liquidity, company value or whether the company can continue trading.

Likewise, positive retained earnings does not prove dividend permission, sufficient usable cash or absence of lender restrictions. Review the actual jurisdiction, governing documents, financing agreements and obligations before a distribution decision. An accounting rollforward answers how the balance moved. A funding or legal-capacity assessment uses additional evidence and should not be replaced by this formula.

Close the reconciliation and retain the evidence

Agree opening balance to the prior close, net income to the final income statement, declarations to authorization and distributions to the liability/payment ledger. List every other movement separately; unresolved differences need an owner and correction trail. Retain the version used for reporting rather than changing last period’s figures silently.

Use the accounting-cycle guide for the full close, accrued-expense schedule for omitted period costs and accounting-equation guide for total-balance context. The statement-analysis guide addresses interpretation after these components reconcile. Retained earnings remains a permanent balance carried forward, rather than an expense account reset to zero each month.

Frequently asked questions

What is the basic retained-earnings formula?

Under the stated base case, opening retained earnings plus net income minus declared dividends equals closing retained earnings. Other supported movements must be shown separately.

Is retained earnings the cash available in the bank?

No. The case closes with $39,000 retained earnings and $23,000 cash. They reconcile different records and can differ substantially.

Do newly issued shares increase retained earnings?

They normally belong in contributed-equity accounts under the applicable treatment, rather than current profit or retained earnings. The example credits the $10,000 share contribution to paid-in capital.

Should paid dividends be deducted again after declaration?

No. The $5,000 declaration already reduces retained earnings. The later $3,000 payment reduces the payable and cash.

Can I use retained earnings to fix any unexplained difference?

No. Determine the cause, period, framework and materiality. A supported opening correction must be separately identified and receive the required treatment.

What does negative retained earnings prove?

It identifies an accumulated deficit under the recorded movements. It does not by itself establish the company’s usable cash, insolvency or distribution rights.
