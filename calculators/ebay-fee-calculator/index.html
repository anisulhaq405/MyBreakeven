How eBay final value fees are calculated

For the supported basic schedules, the percentage applies to the item price, modeled buyer shipping and sales tax. Apply the selected category rate to the first $7,500 per item, then 2.35% to the portion above it. The custom option uses a single flat rate and does not apply a tier.

The model adds $0.30 per order at a checkout total of $10 or less and $0.40 above $10. Sales tax can move a sale above that cutoff even if the item price alone stays below it. Seller revenue excludes buyer tax.

Add actual insertion fees or listing upgrades you want the sale to recover. For an attributed General campaign sale, enter the percentage charged for that listing. The calculator applies it to the modeled checkout total. Check your advertising report for the actual attribution.

What changes near the $10 and $7,500 thresholds?

At $9.50 plus 6% tax and no shipping, the checkout total is $10.07. The order therefore uses the $0.40 charge. A small price increase at the threshold can reduce what you keep temporarily, because the fixed charge jumps by $0.10. The target-price calculation checks both sides.

On the 13.6% schedule, an $8,000 fee base uses 13.6% on the first $7,500 and 2.35% on the remaining $500. That gives $1,031.75 in percentage fees before the order fee. Applying 13.6% to all $8,000 would overstate the modeled fee.

Model an eligible electronically delivered eBay item

Check eBay’s electronically delivered items policy before choosing a listing format. For sellers without approval, eBay directs covered listings to the Information Products classified-ad format. Policy exceptions and approval requirements depend on the product.

Choose Digital product to estimate a one-sale scenario without shipping. Enter only the percentage, fixed, listing and advertising charges that actually apply to the format. For a classified listing using a separate payment method, allocate any applicable classified listing cost and enter the processor’s verified fees on the stated buyer-total basis.

Check the actual category and order before choosing a price

  1. Find the category in the listing and verify whether your Store subscription changes its rate. Choose only a supported preset or a confirmed single flat-rate schedule.
  2. Use the shipping amount eBay uses in the fee base. Expedited and international options can have exceptions. If your order needs separate fee bases, use the eBay statement instead of this model.
  3. Compare your fee statement, ad attribution and net order proceeds. Treat monthly Store cost, fee credits and multi-item checkouts as separate reconciliation items.

Assumptions and limits

One item and one order on eBay US in USD. Physical orders use standard domestic shipping. For an eligible digital sale, only entered fees apply. Rates cover the selected basic category schedules only. The model excludes Store-specific tiered rates, special categories, international fees, account surcharges, multi-item checkouts and exact refund credits. Contribution is before overhead and income tax.

Frequently asked questions

How much does eBay take from a $100 sale?

The worked example uses $8 buyer-paid shipping, 6% tax, a 13.6% category fee and an attributed 4% ad. The modeled eBay fees are $20.55 before seller costs. Changing category, shipping, tax or advertising changes the answer.

Does eBay charge final value fees on sales tax?

The US basic fee schedule uses a total sale amount including applicable sales tax and other covered amounts. The tax is excluded from seller revenue in the profit calculation.

Is the eBay order fee $0.30 or $0.40?

Under the modeled basic schedule it is $0.30 at a total sale amount of $10 or less and $0.40 above $10. Some special category rules differ.

Can I estimate fees for an eBay digital product?

Yes, for an eligible listing. Switch to Digital product and enter verified fees for its listing and payment format. The digital estimate excludes shipping and does not use the physical category fee schedule.

Can I use this for my eBay Store rate?

Use the custom percentage only if the official schedule you verified is a single flat percentage and the rest of the modeled charges apply. Store tiers with different thresholds require a separate reconciliation.
