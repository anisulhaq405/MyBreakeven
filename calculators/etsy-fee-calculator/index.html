How Etsy seller fees are calculated

Start with the item price after seller discounts, then add what you charge for shipping and any gift wrapping. For this US example, Etsy calculates the 6.5% transaction fee on that amount. Etsy Payments charges 3% plus $0.25 on the modeled checkout total, including applicable sales tax. Buyer tax is kept out of your revenue.

Allocate $0.20 per listing charge. A new listing or renewal can incur a charge independently of a sale. If unsold listings or renewals are material, spread those related costs across expected sales instead of treating every order as a single charge.

On an attributed Offsite Ads order, choose the rate Etsy applies to your shop. The modeled fee is 12% or 15% of seller revenue, limited to $100 per order. The calculator compares the same order with and without attribution so you can see what the ad costs.

Calculate profit on an Etsy digital download

Choose Digital product for a printable, template or similar downloadable item. Buyer-paid shipping, postage and packaging are zero. Enter royalties or delivery costs per order and a support or unrecovered refund allowance. To recover a one-time design cost, divide it by a realistic number of sales and include it once in the unit cost.

A fictional $12 printable with 8% effective buyer sales tax, one $0.20 listing charge, no attributed ad and $2.50 in seller costs leaves $7.88 in modeled contribution. The buyer pays $12.96. The 6.5% transaction fee uses the $12 item revenue and the payment processing fee uses the $12.96 checkout total.

Price a handmade item when shipping or advertising changes

  1. Audit two recent payment-account orders. Compare your buyer shipping charge with the actual label and check which sale was attributed to Offsite Ads.
  2. Enter materials and paid making time in landed item cost. Enter packaging, postage and expected unrecovered returns once each.
  3. Test a buyer-paid shipping order and a free shipping offer. With free shipping, set the buyer charge to zero and leave the postage you pay in costs.
  4. Compare attributed and unattributed contribution. Use your actual expected order mix to decide whether the price can fund monthly overhead.

Reconcile Etsy proceeds before changing your prices

Match a single order to the listing, transaction, payment and Offsite Ads entries on its statement. Deposit timing, subscription charges, Etsy Ads spend and other account-level adjustments belong in the monthly plan.

If an order is refunded, check which fees were credited and how much stock was recovered. Use the unrecovered amount to estimate the average loss per completed sale. Keep that estimate separate from the order subtotal.

Assumptions and limits

One domestic US Etsy item with USD settlement and Etsy Payments. Use the effective tax on item plus buyer-paid shipping, keeping the same effective rate in price scenarios. The model excludes currency conversion, taxes on fees, Etsy Ads campaign spend, refunds and separate account adjustments unless you enter an appropriate cost allowance. Contribution is before overhead and income tax.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Etsy take from a $40 sale?

With $5 buyer-paid shipping and 8% effective sales tax, this example has $4.83 in modeled fees before Offsite Ads. An attributed 15% ad adds $6.75. Shipping and sales tax can change the fee bases.

Does the Etsy calculator support digital downloads?

Yes. Choose Digital product to exclude shipping, postage and packaging while keeping the listing, transaction, Etsy Payments and applicable Offsite Ads fees. Use the effective buyer tax shown for your order.

Does Etsy charge fees on shipping?

For this US model, shipping charged to the buyer enters the transaction, payments and applicable Offsite Ads fee bases. Your actual postage is a separate seller cost.

What is the Etsy Offsite Ads cap?

The modeled fee is capped at $100 per attributed order. Select the 12% or 15% rate shown by Etsy for your shop. Use zero for an unattributed order.

Can I use the break-even price as my final listing price?

It covers the entered fees and direct costs. Add a target contribution to fund overhead and owner pay, then compare the result with actual statement fees and buyer demand.
