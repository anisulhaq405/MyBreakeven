Calculate a fixed-rate mortgage payment

Loan principal equals home price less down payment. For monthly rate i and n monthly payments, principal-and-interest payment M = P × i ÷ [1 − (1 + i)^−n]. At zero interest, M = P ÷ n. The annual note rate is divided by 12; APR includes other borrowing costs and is not substituted here.

For each month, interest equals opening principal × monthly rate. The remainder of the loan payment reduces principal. Calculations retain precision; figures display to cents and the last payment clears the exact remaining balance. Actual lender schedules can differ because of daily accrual, rounding or payment dates.

Reproduce the fictional example

A $350,000 price less $70,000 down leaves $280,000 principal. At an assumed 6.5% fixed annual note rate over 30 years, monthly principal and interest are $1,769.79. Annual property tax $4,200 and annual homeowners insurance $1,800 add $350 and $150 per month. With $0 mortgage insurance and $100 HOA, the total monthly ownership budget is $2,369.79. These are inputs, not current rates or local cost benchmarks.

For a separate zero-rate check, $120,000 over 10 years requires $1,000 principal per month, $0 interest and a zero balance after 120 payments. Adding ownership costs does not change amortization or the loan’s interest total.

Loan payment, escrow and ownership costs are different

Principal and interest are the loan-payment component. The lender payment may also collect tax and insurance through escrow and include mortgage insurance. HOA is often paid separately; it is included here only in the broader ownership budget. Do not enter insurance twice.

The full ownership budget can also need maintenance, utilities, closing costs and other charges outside this tool. Taxes and insurance can change even when the principal-and-interest payment stays fixed. This model does not automatically cancel mortgage insurance or estimate approval, home appreciation, tax deductions or refinance savings.

Assumptions and limits

Educational fixed-rate monthly amortization. Inputs stay in this browser tab. No live quotes, approval, affordability determination, tax advice or lender endorsement. No extra payments, variable rates, balloon structures, daily accrual or fee-financing model. Ownership-cost assumptions stay constant; loan-payment totals exclude those separate costs.

Frequently asked questions

Does this show the whole monthly housing cost?

It shows principal and interest plus your entered tax, insurance, mortgage insurance and HOA. Maintenance, utilities and closing costs remain outside the displayed budget.

Should I use APR or the note rate?

Enter the annual fixed note rate. APR incorporates additional borrowing costs and is not the periodic interest rate used by this amortization formula.

Are the default rates current mortgage offers?

No. All default amounts and rates are fictional planning inputs. Obtain current written quotes and property-specific estimates separately.

Does a zero interest rate work?

Yes. The loan principal is divided evenly across the scheduled number of monthly payments.

Does mortgage insurance stop automatically?

No. The entered monthly amount remains constant. Cancellation depends on your loan, servicing and applicable requirements.

Is this the same as the refinance calculator?

No. This calculates an original fixed-rate payment and ownership budget. The refinance tool compares an existing loan and replacement financing, costs and remaining balances at a common exit date.
