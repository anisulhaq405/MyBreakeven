How to use this calculator

  1. Enter both candidate jobs with the same cost and time definitions.
  2. Allocate each driving leg once; enter total journey distance and time.
  3. Include helper wages separately, but leave your own target earnings outside expenses.
  4. Compare hourly earnings and the additional surcharge needed to reach your target.

Formula and calculation boundaries

Occupied hours = (service + setup + allocated travel minutes) ÷ 60. Costs = distance × vehicle cost per unit + direct costs + allocated overhead.

Owner earnings = (base price + existing surcharge) × (1 − fee rate) − entered costs. Hourly earnings = owner earnings ÷ occupied hours.

Required total charge = (entered costs + occupied hours × target hourly earnings) ÷ (1 − fee rate). Additional surcharge = maximum of zero and required total minus current total, rounded upward to cents. Compare hourly returns using unrounded values.

What to check before acting on the result

Compare total owner earnings alongside earnings per occupied hour. A shorter lower-ticket job can rank higher hourly while leaving fewer total dollars.

The extra surcharge assumes customers accept it and the occupied time stays unchanged. Review demand and job scope before changing a quote.

If several workers are involved, include helper labor as a direct cost and use the owner-time boundary consistently; this is not a multi-crew scheduling model.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use kilometers?

Yes. Use kilometers and vehicle cost per kilometer for both jobs. Distance is total allocated travel, not automatically doubled.

Why not use fuel cost alone?

Vehicle cost may include wear, maintenance and insurance allocations. Do not also include the same vehicle costs in overhead.

Is owner hourly earnings net profit?

No. It includes compensation for the owner’s time and is before personal tax and unentered costs.

Does the suggested extra surcharge include fees?

Yes. It grosses up the target charge for the entered percentage payment fee, then subtracts what you already charge.

Can I enter only one-way travel?

Enter the complete distance and occupied travel time allocated to the job, including the return or relevant route legs. The tool does not automatically double the distance or time.
