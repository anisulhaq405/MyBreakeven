Founder, MyBreakeven

Anis Ul Haq is the founder of MyBreakeven, a business planning website for owner-operators. Its calculators connect prices and direct costs with monthly commitments, customer demand and delivery capacity.

The purpose of the product

A sales target is more useful when you can inspect both the calculation and the work needed to deliver it. MyBreakeven lets owners change their own assumptions and see which constraint moves the answer.

The site publishes industry guides alongside its Free and Pro tools. Example inputs illustrate how a model works; they do not represent a verified market rate or a customer's achieved result.
