Why MyBreakeven

A financial target is more useful when you can inspect both the calculation and the work needed to deliver it. MyBreakeven lets business owners change their assumptions and explore costs, required sales, customer demand and delivery capacity.

Its worked examples explain the models; they are not forecasts or verified customer results.
