Break-even and feasibility analysis

MyBreakeven combines unit economics with team capacity and conversion assumptions so business owners can test whether a monthly sales target is financially and operationally feasible.

How many customers do you need to break even?

Illustrative service-business example: a $200 job with $95 in direct costs and a $5 payment fee contributes $100. With $2,400 monthly overhead and $1,600 owner pay, 40 jobs ($8,000 sales) cover those costs. A $1,000 profit target needs 50 jobs ($10,000 sales) and 200 inquiries at 25% conversion.

Two people working 20 hours each per week at 50% productive utilization have about 87 delivery hours per month. At two hours per job, they can complete only 43 whole jobs, so the 50-job profit target exceeds capacity. These are sample inputs, not industry averages. See the SBA break-even formula.
