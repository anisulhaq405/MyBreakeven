Method and controls

Define planned value, earned value and actual cost

Planned value is the approved budget for work scheduled by the status date. Earned value is the approved budget for work actually completed by that date, using a defensible completion method. Actual cost records what that completed work has cost on a consistent accounting basis. Match scope, cut-off and currency before dividing.

CPI = EV ÷ AC. Below 1 means the measured work has consumed more actual cost than its budgeted value; above 1 means the opposite. A zero actual-cost denominator needs investigation, not a fabricated finite index. Missing supplier accruals can make CPI look artificially favorable.

Test the remaining efficiency needed to meet a chosen budget

CPI describes cost performance achieved to date. The separate to-complete performance index, or TCPI, asks what efficiency the remaining work would need to meet a selected funding target. PMI’s TCPI paper explains the remaining-work numerator and the different denominators for the original budget and an approved revised estimate.

Using the existing fictional status, $50,000 of budgeted work remains: $100,000 BAC minus $50,000 EV. Only $37,500 remains within the original budget: $100,000 minus $62,500 AC. Thus TCPI to the original BAC is $50,000 / $37,500 = 1.3333. That would require substantially better remaining cost efficiency than the measured 0.80 CPI; it is a requirement, not proof the team can achieve it.

If the selected revised target is the $125,000 EAC, its remaining funding is $62,500 and TCPI becomes $50,000 / $62,500 = 0.80. That matches the continued-efficiency assumption used to derive this EAC. A $120,500 bottom-up EAC gives $58,000 remaining funding and TCPI approximately 0.8621. Each denominator must identify the target being tested; changing the forecast does not erase the original approved baseline.

When the selected target has no remaining funding, this ratio is undefined or infeasible for positive remaining work. Investigate scope, accrual cutoff, remaining commitments and corrective resources before using the result. Do not interpret 1.3333 as a required percentage margin, a promised productivity improvement or a schedule completion date.
