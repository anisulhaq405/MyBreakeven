Method and controls

Connect a risk to a date and an accountable action

Choose a planning horizon that captures your payment commitments. List opening usable cash, expected receipts and committed payments by week or date. Separate restricted balances, unapproved borrowing and expected insurance recoveries from cash available to pay bills. Identify which receipts depend on customer acceptance, processing delays or unresolved disputes.

For each risk, record the event, a measurable trigger, the owner, the response deadline, the action’s cash effect and the dependency that could prevent it. A reserve target should follow the business’s downside timing and tolerance, not an unexplained percentage copied from another company.

Turn the four-week closing balance into a dated funding test

The SBA emergency-preparedness material covers risk assessment, a business-specific response plan and practice. It supports preparation; it does not establish this example’s $5,000 cash floor or promise financing. Those amounts must follow the business’s actual commitments and decision policy.

A positive month-end balance can conceal an earlier shortage. Extend the fictional base with dated movements: begin at $18,000, pay $12,000 in week one, then $8,000 in week two, and receive $8,000 in week four. Closing cash is still $6,000, but week-two cash reaches −$2,000. Restoring the selected $5,000 floor before that payment requires $7,000 of timely additional cash or agreed payment changes, rather than the $1,000 month-end buffer suggested by the aggregate base.

Apply the same timing test to each response. If a $4,000 credit draw can arrive only in week three, it does not solve the week-two payment. If a supplier agrees to defer $1,500, record the replacement due date and include it in the next forecast period. Distinguish removing a genuinely uncommitted purchase from merely postponing an existing obligation.

Keep the last evidence date beside every available-funding line. Check draw conditions, approvals and settlement delays before treating it as usable. Assign an alternative action if any condition fails, and rehearse the decision before the critical payment date. Disaster assistance, insurance claims and hoped-for receivable collections remain conditional until their amount and timing are supported.
