Keep the numbers you want to revisit

  1. Enter and check your own inputs in the full calculator. From an industry calculator, choose “Continue with these numbers”.
  2. Sign in and choose Save scenario. A free account can keep up to 3; Pro supports up to 100.
  3. For Pro comparisons, save distinct cases and select up to 3 in your dashboard. Use the same unit and currency.

Opening pricing or checkout does not save or transfer an unsaved plan. The Monthly Break-Even Monitor keeps its entries in the current browser; saved account scenarios are separate.
