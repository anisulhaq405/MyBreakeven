## Calculation boundary

Original booking cohort N; disjoint no-shows and late cancellations sum to M. Completed replacements R must fit those original missed slots; R ≤ M ≤ N. Original and replacement contribution each equal ticket × (1 − effective service processing %) − fixed service transaction charge − full variable service cost. One transaction per completed service is assumed. No demand displacement is modeled.

Gap = M × original contribution − R × replacement contribution + actual incremental waste − (gross fees retained after refunds − their actual payment/refund costs). Full original-service variable cost is counterfactual; waste is actual missed-booking cost and must not also be in actual replacement costs. Unchanged fixed rent and salaries cancel out.
