# Real business walkthrough intake

Status: preparation complete; two real walkthroughs await owners, records and publication permission. Do not publish the hypothetical demo as a real business case. Do not promise an achieved improvement from a planning result.

## Owner consent record

Record: owner identity/contact privately, business model, records supplied, permitted date range, permitted name/anonymization, allowed quoted statements/images, permission to publish the exact final text, date permission obtained. No customer names, banking details, passwords or identifiable employee payroll should enter the public case. A draft agreement is a workflow record, not legal advice.

## Cleaning quote versus actual job

Collect job date/scope, estimate and actual invoice revenue on the same tax basis, estimated and actual total paid crew-hours, loaded hourly rate, supplies used, travel/equipment cost, fee calculation and any other direct costs. Identify source documents and missing entries. Keep owner delivery labor treatment explicit.

Recompute estimated and actual direct costs and contribution. Explain differences line by line. Do not call contribution final net profit. If a revised quote is proposed, label it proposed; track a subsequent comparable job before claiming an outcome. Have the owner check the factual draft and approve exact publication.

Public structure: decision → documented period and scope → source/permission note → inputs table → reproducible calculation → what changed → decision taken → observed result or “not yet measured” → limits → one relevant calculator/resource.

## Agency retainer versus delivered scope

Collect contracted client-period, revenue, included scope, agreed revision/meeting limits, planned and actual delivery worker-hours, loaded labor/contractor cost, per-client software, transaction fees, acquisition-cost treatment, changes billed and collected, and productive monthly capacity. Separate fixed overhead and owner pay from per-client costs to avoid double counting.

Recompute contribution before overhead and monthly target/capacity separately. Distinguish recurring invoices, cash received and delivered work. A proposed change order is not earned revenue. Record whether the owner actually changed scope or price and whether a later period can establish an outcome.

Use the same public structure as above, with owner fact-check and publication approval. Identify professional review by actual name, permission and scope only if it occurs.

## Intake questionnaire to share after authorization

1. Which decision do you want to inspect, and which completed job/client-month is comparable?
2. Which input records can you provide in redacted form?
3. Which costs or time entries are missing or estimated?
4. What was actually changed, and what outcome has been observed?
5. May the exact approved case be published with your name, or should it be anonymized?

Do not solicit records or contact an owner without the relevant authorization and verified recipient.
