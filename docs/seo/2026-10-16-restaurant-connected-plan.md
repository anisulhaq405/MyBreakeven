# Restaurant calculator journey — October 16, 2026

The recipe-to-menu handoff already existed. It remains itemized; now it expires after five minutes, is consumed once, and identifies missing/expired handoffs instead of silently presenting example inputs as carried costs. Inventory-period usage is deliberately not mapped to a per-item ingredient cost: actual sold recipe mix is required for a comparable theoretical standard.

Menu pricing now explains the next action for nonpositive contribution, a capacity gap, a funding shortfall, or a feasible assumption-based scenario. Food-period results link to recipe standards or reconciliation guidance as appropriate. These explanations use existing engine results and do not change formulas or default numbers.

An expandable result-panel handoff carries the tested menu price, ingredients, variable labor, packaging plus other costs, fees, fixed costs, owner compensation, profit target, expected monthly orders and selected currency into the full restaurant calculator. It requires explicit confirmation of a complete representative order and matching monthly cost scope. Six operating fields start blank: additional promotion cost, people, weekly hours, worker-hours per order, utilization and inquiry conversion. Menu weekly unit capacity is not converted into invented hours; the full calculator recomputes capacity. A single dish must not be treated as a complete restaurant order. Financial inputs stay in same-tab session storage, expire after five minutes, and never enter the URL or analytics. Storage failure blocks navigation and keeps inputs in place.

Measurement: consent-gated calculator_use/calculator_result_ready carry only an allowlisted food-tool slug and restaurant industry. recipe_menu_continue and menu_plan_continue record explicit successful staging actions. save_scenario_open and save_login_click distinguish intent from scenario_saved (only after successful database insertion). signup_request_accepted means an accepted signup request, not verified registration, login completion or a sale. Edited-result tracking also retries after analytics initialization, not just the consent signal. No financial values, ingredient names, order details or customer identifiers are added to product analytics. These events need future real usage before funnel conclusions can be drawn.
