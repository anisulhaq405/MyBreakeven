# Agency cluster — 10 October 2026

Hub: `/guides/agency-business/`. Thirteen existing guides, five decision groups, three calculator destinations: agency-break-even-calculator, hourly-rate-calculator and cash-runway-calculator. Hourly rate and runway remain shared cross-industry tools; no exclusive agency navigation is imposed on them. Thirteen guide pages and the agency calculator link into the hub through templates; the blogs hub links in.

Intent ownership is stored in `src/agencyCluster.js`. Launch cost, freelance financial planning and ongoing margin describe different decisions. Retainer pricing sets scoped work, project-versus-retainer compares engagements and client profitability diagnoses a particular account. Scope creep measures unpaid delivery; staffing comparison tests equal workloads. Utilization measures available productive hours, client targets translate required contribution and break-even joins monthly commitments. Concentration compares revenue/contribution dependence; the cash forecast checks dated collections and payments. The general cash-flow guide keeps its wider audience and existing URL.

No new article is justified from inventory alone. Existing article bodies, dates and URLs are preserved. Search volume, keyword difficulty, country SERP results and GSC query ownership are unverified in this preparation. Do not claim complete topical coverage, low competition, indexing or traffic gains.
