## Gap decisions and overlap guard

Existing profitability and weekly-job guides mention weather/cancellations; the travel-fee guide explains changed allocation when a route loses a stop. They do not provide a full planned-versus-revised route reconciliation across early cancellation, late notice, actual replacement and committed-shift pay. The new cancellation guide owns that narrow event task. It does not prescribe customer fees, deposit retention or legal policy.

Existing startup, fleet and weekly-job guides mention water supply, filling and setup. The new refill guide adds ordered water availability, complete refill cost, mixed-service demand and whole-job time limits. It does not recommend tank size, payload changes, installation, cleaning methods or wastewater handling. Chemical dilution remains with its existing calculator.

Hold generic "detailing pricing", "detailing travel profit" and "detailing business startup guide" duplicates: those intents have existing owners. No broad rewrites of recently updated content are needed for this cluster. Normal semantic overlap in terms such as cost, travel and jobs is expected; avoid competing pages for the same decision.
