# FIRE and Coast FIRE calculator research and scope

User priority overrides the earlier sequence: complete seven calculators first, supporting articles later. EBITDA and Future Value are live. This release adds only /calculators/fire-calculator/ and /calculators/coast-fire-calculator/. Financial Leverage will be a Financial Ratios mode, not an eighth calculator. No links to unbuilt guides.

Research completed before implementation October 26, 2026. WalletBurst FIRE and Coast tools expose distinct accumulation vs stop-contributing tasks and charts; their pages describe an approximate nominal-minus-inflation real rate. Networthify foregrounds savings and time to financial independence. Engaging Data offers fixed-rate, historical and Monte Carlo methods. Do not pretend our deterministic scenarios have comparable probabilistic coverage or validated success probabilities.

Benchmarks checked: https://walletburst.com/tools/fire-calculator/ ; https://walletburst.com/tools/coast-fire-calc/ ; https://networthify.com/calculator/earlyretirement ; https://engaging-data.com/fire-calculator/ . No live US rank-position claim is made. Supplied private FIRE SERP snapshot contains unrelated game and localized app results; it is not proof of easy ranking.

Primary methods: OpenStax Fisher relationship https://openstax.org/books/principles-finance-2e/pages/7-4-applications-of-tvm-in-finance states (1+nominal)=(1+real)(1+inflation). Investor.gov fees bulletin https://www.investor.gov/introduction-investing/general-resources/news-alerts/alerts-bulletins/investor-bulletins/updated explains compounding fee effects. OpenStax annuities https://openstax.org/books/principles-finance/pages/8-2-annuities provides cash-flow timing basis. Morningstar public search extracts distinguish 30-year assumed retirement horizons and model assumptions; its article fetch returned 403, so do not reproduce research tables, prescribe its rate or claim independent access to the full paper.

Contract: return is nominal effective annual before the entered annual asset fee; net annual factor=(1+return)*(1-fee); real factor=net factor/(1+inflation). Monthly effective factor=real factor^(1/12). Fee model is disclosed, not a product fee quote. All balances, spending and contributions are in today's money. Fixed-real monthly savings imply nominal deposits rise with inflation. Do not double-adjust inputs or add a fee already included in a net return.

FIRE target=annual portfolio-funded retirement spending/withdrawal fraction. Exact first whole month meeting target, funded ratio and gap; selected-age required contribution uses a separate end-month annuity calculation. Schedule stops at first target attainment or selected horizon; it is accumulation, not post-retirement drawdown. Explicit already reached, not reached, no-time and supported-range states. Zero spending is permitted and explicitly means no portfolio-funded spending, not no lifetime expenses.

Coast threshold=FIRE target/(real factor)^years until retirement. Core status always assumes zero further deposits. Optional contribution scenario stays separate and cannot relabel core Coast status. At zero remaining years threshold=FIRE target; negative real growth can make threshold exceed target. Current living costs still need funding.

Original verification: 40,000 spending at4%=1,000,000 FIRE target. 100,000 plus1,000 monthly at zero net real growth reaches200,000 in100months. Coast1,000,000 at5%real20years=376,889.48. Independent schedules, negative/zero growth, fee/inflation rate algebra, contribution monotonicity, missing values, target ages, zero spending and CSV checked. Scenario return comparisons are not confidence intervals.
