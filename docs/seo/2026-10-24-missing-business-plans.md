## Calculation scope and verification

All examples are fictional USD assumptions for January–December 2027. No demand forecast, market benchmark, lender approval or independent accountant review is claimed.

Landscaping: eight active months produce conditional $64,000 revenue and $4,000 result before excluded costs. The original 100-visit target fails 85-visit capacity. Reducing each active month to 85 visits yields a $2,000 annual loss before excluded costs. The plan explicitly requires correction before commitments.

Agency: 8 opening clients minus 1 lost plus 2 new at the month start gives 9 billed clients, $18,000 revenue, $3,900 result and 180 required versus 208 available worker-hours. Half-current/half-next-month collections reduce January cash to $2,900 from $8,000 despite positive period result.

Ecommerce: 500 shipped one-unit orders leave $6,125 contribution and $1,125 after $5,000 fixed costs. January closing cash is $16,625 after $12,000 stock payment and 90% current-month settlement. The $4,500 profit/cash difference reconciles to $2,000 inventory increase plus $2,500 unsettled sales. Returned stock is assumed non-resalable; the returns allowance is also paid in the same month. Costs used are the entered per-unit planning costs, not a FIFO inventory valuation system.
