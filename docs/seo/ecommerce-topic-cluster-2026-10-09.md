# E-commerce cluster — 9 October 2026

Hub: `/guides/ecommerce-business/`. Sixteen existing guides, six decision groups, three calculator destinations: ecommerce-break-even-calculator, ecommerce-return-cost-calculator and break-even-roas-calculator. ROAS is a cross-industry tool: it retains its broad scope; no exclusive ecommerce parent is imposed. The return-cost page and industry calculator link into the hub, as do all sixteen guide templates and the blogs hub.

The guide registry `src/ecommerceCluster.js` assigns each destination once. Startup funding differs from ongoing margin; product pricing differs from bundle discounting; shipping costs differ from threshold offer economics. Returns and chargebacks have distinct refund, fee and recovered-stock outcomes. Marketplace comparison, paid acquisition and subscription churn each own a different decision. Stock holding and reorder timing distinguish inventory cost from payment timing. Daily orders translate monthly break-even into a fulfillment target. Physical retail remains a related model with different receipts/staffing rather than an identical ecommerce formula.

No new blog is justified by inventory alone. Existing bodies, URLs and dates are preserved. Verified search volume, keyword difficulty, country SERP evidence and GSC query ownership are unavailable in this preparation. This map is not evidence of low competition or future traffic.
