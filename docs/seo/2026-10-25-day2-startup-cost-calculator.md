# Original Day 2 — general Business Startup Cost Calculator

New owner: `/calculators/business-startup-cost-calculator/`. This implements the original general launch-spending, reserve and funding-gap task. It does not create another ecommerce cost guide, cash runway calculator, dated cash forecast or startup valuation owner.

The model separates total launch cash, already-paid amounts and unpaid cash due before month one. Constant monthly cash commitments plus month-specific payments are reconciled against customer cash collections over 1–24 months. The deepest cumulative trading deficit, selected minimum cash floor and contingency on unpaid launch costs produce the requirement; available unrestricted cash produces the gap or excess. Already-paid costs remain visible but are not charged against the after-payment cash balance twice. Month-end netting, horizon limits and omitted payments are explicit.

Service and ecommerce examples are fictional. The zero-receipts stress action preserves costs. CSV exports the current input lists, funding bridge and monthly cash path as values, with spreadsheet-safe custom labels. Currency labels do not exchange values. No Excel formula workbook, demand forecast, funding approval or tax treatment is promised.
