## Eight ready-to-share resource drafts

### Cleaning

A cleaning quote can cover supplies and still leave too little for paid crew time and travel. Start with the complete visit cost, then check how many completed visits your monthly commitments need. These guides connect contract bids, recurring work, job actuals and break-even. Use your own inputs rather than an assumed local rate.

https://mybreakeven.com/guides/cleaning-business/

### Landscaping

A higher job price does not recover hours lost on a scattered route. Compare crew time, equipment use and job contribution before forecasting the month. The landscaping guides cover routes, maintenance scope, seasonality and capacity with worked planning examples.

https://mybreakeven.com/guides/landscaping-business/

### Mobile detailing

The ticket price is only the start of a mobile detail. Setup, pack-down, travel and a changed route all affect what the working day leaves. Use these guides to cost packages, fleet visits, cancellations and water-refill downtime before checking monthly capacity.

https://mybreakeven.com/guides/mobile-detailing-business/

### Salon

Membership collections, completed appointments and stylist hours are different numbers. Cost the service your customer can redeem, then check the diary alongside regular appointments. The salon guides connect service prices, no-shows, retail contribution and break-even.

https://mybreakeven.com/guides/salon-business/

### Restaurant

Food cost percentage alone does not show what an order leaves. Packaging, channel fees, paid delivery work and service capacity also matter. Explore menu pricing, takeout comparisons, catering scope and restaurant break-even with explicit planning assumptions.

https://mybreakeven.com/guides/restaurant-business/

### Photography

Camera time is only part of a photography assignment. Include preparation, editing, revisions and delivery before comparing packages or setting a monthly client target. These guides cover headshots, product work, second shooters, print packages and capacity.

https://mybreakeven.com/guides/photography-business/

### E-commerce

An order can leave positive contribution while inventory purchases strain cash. Keep product costs, shipping, fees, returns and acquisition visible, then check stock payment timing separately. These guides connect order economics to fulfillment capacity and break-even.

https://mybreakeven.com/guides/ecommerce-business/

### Agency

A retainer invoice can hide extra meetings, revisions and coordination. Cost the complete scope, inspect client contribution and compare required work with productive team hours. The agency guides cover pricing, scope changes, utilization, client concentration and cash timing.

https://mybreakeven.com/guides/agency-business/
