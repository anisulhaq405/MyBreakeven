## Scope and search limitation

## Method

Reviewed current resolved article bodies, existing examples, FAQs, navigation and linked tools. Read the comparison-page headings and relevant body sections. Retrieved public pages read-only on 10 November 2026; methods were cross-checked against primary publisher material including SEC, OpenStax, university teaching material and official Amazon pricing. A direct HDFC fetch returned 403, but its content was available through the search service. Khan Academy/MSU pages with empty extraction were replaced by readable Illustrative Mathematics tasks. No paywall or CAPTCHA was bypassed.

The examples and additions are original teaching material, not copied competitor examples, current local price benchmarks, investment recommendations or new field-study results. Brief observations below do not endorse unverified competitor price/statistical claims. Competitor strengths suggest useful intent coverage; that is an editorial inference rather than a ranking diagnosis. Existing articles already had substantial worked examples, so the changes target remaining decision gaps instead of replacing strong original ledgers.
