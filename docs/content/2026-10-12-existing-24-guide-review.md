## Accuracy corrections

- Ecommerce: removed unsupported margin ranges; distinguished operating and net profit; reconciled recognized sales versus collections; removed the claim that projected operating profit establishes a safe owner distribution. Shopify reporting boundaries are explained without inventing current plan or processing fees.
- Gross versus contribution margin: fixed production overhead can be included in COGS; contribution can legitimately exceed gross margin. Both routes reconcile to the same operating profit in the example.
- Inventory holding: the $90 non-capital amount includes modeled allowances/allocations and is no longer described as necessarily actual cash paid.
- Lawn pricing: percentage fees belong in the price equation; equipment cash, fixed commitments and usage-related costs have separate boundaries.
- Existing editorial links were retained, including marketplace comparison and subscription churn links detected by the repository's content-recovery test.
- Changed FAQ answers are reconciled in the source FAQ data and inline content where present.
