## Material corrections

1. `how-to-start-online-business`: a fictional inquiry log was labeled **Observed pilot list**, and prose called it an observed pilot. Replace those phrases with explicitly fictional labels. The introductory disclosure alone should not be contradicted by an evidence-column label.
2. `current-assets` and `current-liabilities`: SEC-hosted 2012/2014 issuer taxonomy pages are not SEC-authored accounting guidance and are not standards. Replace them with educational primary-source links, explicitly describe a short-cycle US introductory case and retain the need for actual framework-specific classification analysis. Do not mix IFRS restrictions/right-to-defer tests into a US example without labeling the framework.
3. `accounts-receivable-process`: **An AR ledger cannot equal the bank balance** is an unjustified absolute; amounts can coincide. Replace with the distinction between the accounts and their separate reconciliations. The JPMorgan URL returned a 403 during retrieval. Replace reliance on its claim with the successfully read Microsoft process documentation and add OpenStax's settlement illustration. A 403 does not establish that the public page is permanently broken.
