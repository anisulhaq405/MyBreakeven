## Critical cross-article terminology

Where a case subtracts **loaded/internal labor** or “relevant cash delivery costs,” the remaining amount is a case-defined delivery margin/surplus; it is textbook CVP contribution only if those costs genuinely vary with the selected activity over the stated range. A salaried person's internally allocated hourly charge can be useful for full-cost quoting but is not automatically avoidable cash or variable cost. Existing articles often disclose boundaries, but the full update should make this distinction explicit, especially the proposal, idea comparison and canvas. Do not imply a stronger accounting definition than the cost assumptions support.

### landscaping-working-capital

**Finding:** No arithmetic error. Weekly-close minimum is deliberately not a daily minimum. The selected cash floor is not an industry standard. BDC is general Canadian education; keep US law/loan questions separate.

**Checked:** base closes $4,000 / −$3,000 / $1,000 / $4,000; receipts $28,000, payments $36,000. Required top-up to chosen $5,000 weekly-close floor $8,000. Receipt delay creates closes $4,000 / −$3,000 / −$5,000 / $4,000 and $10,000 top-up.

**Substantive addition — “Reconcile the funding draw and its later repayment”:**

> Treat the amount needed to protect the chosen floor as an output of a dated cash schedule. If an $8,000 draw is inserted immediately before the base case's lowest weekly close, that close rises from −$3,000 to $5,000. The borrowing does not create sales or operating profit. Its later principal repayment, interest and fees must appear on their actual payment dates. Do not draw $8,000 in every subsequent week simply because the forecast once showed an $8,000 requirement. Recompute the balance after each actual movement. A lender may impose availability conditions, security or covenants; the forecast does not establish that an application is approved. Use the chosen lender's written terms and the actual bank balance. Extend the schedule beyond the seasonal peak so a repayment does not create the next shortfall.

Source for general cash-date principle: BDC cash forecasting above. Numerical draw illustration is new original arithmetic, not a source statistic.

**Substantive addition — “Keep a demand-evidence record before comparing income”:**

> For each candidate idea, record the precise customer group, the problem they currently pay to solve, the alternative supplier, the offered scope, and the date of each conversation or paid test.
