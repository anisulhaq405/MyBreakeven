## Chosen model
One comparable completed job / maintenance visit. Variable employee payroll and paid route time are separate from committed payroll. Owner compensation is counted once as a monthly allocation; owner hours still constrain delivery. Total equipment machine-hours × chosen use-related allowance is a distinct cost, not automatically multiplied by employees. Avoid repeating fuel, depreciation, rental or reserves across lines. Debt principal/capital purchases need cash planning.

Calendar-month thresholds cover one month. The separate seasonal recovery assumes all 12 months of the entered fixed costs, owner allocation and extra goal, zero off-season contribution and 1–12 active months. Seasonal full jobs per active month = annual full requirement / (active months × unrounded job contribution), with whole-job ceilings applied last. Report annual minimum and constant rounded monthly pace separately. Both target and planned volume require capacity checks.
