# MyBreakeven keyword foundation — 25 September 2026

Scope: English-language owner-operators, US first; Canada, UK and Australia measured separately. This is a research and rollout register, not a claim that any term has a verified low organic difficulty or a known monthly search volume. The phrases below are exact strings to test, not exact-match stuffing instructions.

## Evidence and limits

- Private Search Console performance data belongs in the owner's private reporting channel, not this public source repository. Review country, page and query signals privately before prioritizing any phrase. Confirm index state again before interpreting rankings.
- The site already has a homepage calculator, eight industry calculators, and guides in startup, pricing, profitability, volume and concepts. Do not create duplicate routes for the same intent.
- Public result reconnaissance found direct tools for service break-even (Toolsque), monthly sales targets (Service Rate Lab), ecommerce break-even (EcomGauge) and cleaning jobs (Tidyly). Specific wording offers a possible relevance advantage but **does not establish low competition**. Broad break-even SERPs include SBA and many established calculators; avoid a broad-keyword-only bet.
- No country-specific Google Ads Keyword Planner or independent organic-difficulty export is available. Leave search volume and difficulty blank rather than inventing them. Google Ads `Competition` describes advertisers, not organic difficulty. Exact geo estimates require country, language, network, date range and close-variant handling recorded with the export.

## Research gate for the final low-competition shortlist

1. Obtain a Keyword Planner export for US, Canada, UK and Australia separately, English, Google Search, trailing 12 months. Capture average monthly search *range*, close variants, trend, CPC and date. Exact per-keyword counts may not be available in the account; preserve the displayed range rather than replacing it with a guessed point estimate.
2. For every candidate with demand, inspect the actual organic results in the target country: task intent, strength of top 10 pages, directly editable tools, page freshness, source quality and SERP features. Google Ads competition is not organic difficulty. Reject pages whose intended URL cannot satisfy the searcher's task.
3. Map each approved primary phrase to one canonical URL and 2–4 natural secondary variants. Reject near-duplicate page proposals. Record estimated opportunity and confidence; allow low-volume high-intent terms when they clearly lead to calculator use.
4. Cross-check weekly GSC page+query+country impressions and calculator starts. A query becoming visible is evidence of discovery, not proof that its overall search volume is high.

## Rollout and review gates

- **Today:** publish nothing automatically. Review the isolated homepage diff: a checked arithmetic example, clear assumptions and source, no title/canonical/robots/redirect edits. Build and verify the server-delivered fallback and interactive version agree.
- **Weeks 1–2:** after country-volume and SERP gate, select at most two existing money pages with distinct intent, verify indexability and improve one at a time. Add descriptive contextual links from relevant guides.
- **Weeks 3–4:** compare page+country+query groups against the recorded baseline, plus calculator starts. Check indexing, not only average position. Revise copy where query intent and landing page disagree.
- **Weeks 5–8:** expand only topics with verified demand and useful original examples. Add dated source-backed data to relevant industry pages: SBA break-even formula; BLS May 2025 occupational wages (published 2026); Census business data; ONS UK business demography; Statistics Canada business counts; ABS 2025–26 business counts. State geography, reference period, definition and link. These statistics describe markets; they are never substituted for a user's actual costs or presented as typical margins.
- **Rollback:** revert the single homepage content commit if layout, calculation, or engagement regresses. Preserve existing URLs, title, canonical, index directives and calculator logic throughout this phase.
